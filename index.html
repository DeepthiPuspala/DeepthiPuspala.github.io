Lung & Colon Cancer Detection

An autoencoder-embedded CNN with histogram equalization that classifies histopathology images into five tissue classes: benign lung, lung adenocarcinoma, lung squamous cell carcinoma, colon adenocarcinoma and benign colon.

  • 25,000 balanced Kaggle histopathology images (5,000 per class), merged from lung and colon sets
  • Histogram equalization for contrast, then an autoencoder feeding a CNN classifier trained with Adam and categorical cross-entropy
  • Reached 0.991 ROC AUC with 0.895 precision and 0.887 recall and accuracy in 10 epochs; also served predictions on newly uploaded images
